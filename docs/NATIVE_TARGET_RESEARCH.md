# Entity targets and cast result correlation

This covers source inspection, mocked preparation and reducer coverage, and the explicitly scoped live samples below. Native production execution remains disabled.

The inspected Script Extender source is pinned to `45f32c1a1fc10b08a962f0463bb5f2cd0cb9cc76`. Its [`SpellCastShared.h`](https://github.com/Norbyte/bg3se/blob/45f32c1a1fc10b08a962f0463bb5f2cd0cb9cc76/BG3Extender/GameDefinitions/Components/SpellCastShared.h) defines `BaseTarget.Target` as an EntityHandle, optional `TargetProxy` and `Position`, and `InitialTarget.TargetingType` as a SpellType. `InitialTarget` also has optional `Target2`; its meaning should not be guessed for multi-target or directional requests.

[`Spell.inl`](https://github.com/Norbyte/bg3se/blob/45f32c1a1fc10b08a962f0463bb5f2cd0cb9cc76/BG3Extender/GameDefinitions/Components/Spell.inl) gives explicit Position priority over entity position, and resolves TargetProxy before Target. These helpers do not establish player knowledge, line of sight, navigation validity, range, target conditions, resource payment or action acceptance. In particular, an EntityHandle that exists is not evidence that an enemy is visible to the bound teammate.

Read-only preparation therefore permits only self-target Shout or a single Target spell directed at a living recruited party entity. It checks current party membership, death state and entity existence in the same evaluation that resolves the actor and SpellBook source. The output's `target` is a descriptive UUID plus `knowledge="recruited_party"` and targeting type; it is not the typed `InitialTarget` array, and must never be appended directly. Targets need not be host-owned because the actor, rather than a friendly target, is the controlled entity. All engine legality remains explicitly unvalidated. Position, nonparty entity, Projectile, Cone, Zone, multi-target and secondary-target adapters remain rejected.

[`SpellCast.h`](https://github.com/Norbyte/bg3se/blob/45f32c1a1fc10b08a962f0463bb5f2cd0cb9cc76/BG3Extender/GameDefinitions/Components/SpellCast.h) defines `CastStartRequest.RequestGuid` separately from `SpellCastState.SpellCastGuid`. The state has no reflected RequestGuid. Both carry a NetGuid string, but a shared field name alone does not prove copying, uniqueness or client synchronization. An arbitrary NetGuid must not be invented as a correlation shortcut. Matching caster, spell and time is a heuristic that can collide with another human action or an engine-generated cast. A request leaving a queue proves only absence on a later bounded sample.

## Limited live self-Shout results

On 2026-10-10, the independent balanced tutorial campaign on BG3 DX11 4.1.1.7631656 / SE 32 produced two traced native NetworkStartRequests samples for recruited, host-owned non-avatar Lae'zel. The Hide sample had equal RequestGuid and SpellCastGuid and returned `CantCast` in both finished and outcome; action points remained 1. Its underlying cause was not established. The Dash sample had RequestGuid and SpellCastGuid `e303973a-ff51-4e35-b3a0-d384665da562`, started at trace tick 2702 and finished/outcome `None` at tick 2703. An independent CastedSpell event reported Shout_Dash (sequence 4); action points changed 1 to 0 and movement changed 9 to 18. The consuming project retains Dash evidence in ignored `artifacts/live/resumed-dash-result.json`.

These samples validate initialized trace callbacks and exact GUID equality for those two self-Shout requests in this runtime. They do not establish equality for all spells, enemy or position targets, interruption paths or multiplayer clients. The statement above about source not proving a mapping still holds; runtime evidence now exists for this bounded case.

Developer-only `research_receipts.reduce_shout_trace` reads at most 96 trace records and requires expected session/actor, exact request/cast GUIDs, one ordered queued/started/finished/outcome lifecycle, a consistent cast entity, and agreeing terminal results. It accepts only the observed Shout_Hide and Shout_Dash scope and known `None` or `CantCast` results. Missing, truncated, errored, ambiguous, mismatched or unknown evidence yields uncertain, with no retry permission. Its completed classification means correlated engine success; it does not itself verify resource changes. This reducer is not integrated into submission or the companion service.

Before production targeted submission, capture a normal targeted cast's complete InitialTarget and flags, verify reflected conversion and same-call actor/target/session checks, and tie its request to the resulting cast entity with tested identity evidence. Validate normal cost payment, failure outcome and multiplayer synchronization. Ambiguous or missing correlations must yield an uncertain receipt without resubmission. The present preparation API does none of those operations.
